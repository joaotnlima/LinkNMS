# LinkNMS — Brand Book

**One record every side trusts.** This is the applied identity for LinkNMS —
the elected logo, the palette, the type, and the interface components, in one
place you can browse and, in future, publish online.

Meaning and governance live in `BRAND.md`; this book is how it looks when it
is used. The written strategy and voice are the source of truth — when they
and a specimen here disagree, the strategy wins.

## The logo is the mark

The elected logo is the **“House Record”** mark: a house whose roof is split at
the ridge into **Owner Blue** and **Builder Orange**, over a paper body — the
record — holding a longer *planned* bar (blue) and a shorter *actual* bar
(orange). The two sides of a build, side by side, over planned vs. actual.

**The icon is the logo.** The wordmark *LinkNMS* and the slogan **“Trust
built-in.”** are *added* only where a surface needs them — each context decides
whether to show the name, and in which arrangement (horizontal or stacked).

## How this book is organised

- **Brand** — Logo (mark, lockups, clear space & misuse), Colour, Typography, Voice.
- **Components** — the interface primitives and record components, token-driven
  and interactive: Button, Form field, Status & party, Record.

## Keeping it alive

Colour, type and spacing come from design tokens (`tokens/tokens.json` in the
repo); the logo assets regenerate from the SVG masters. Edit the source, rebuild,
and this book — and the product — follow. See `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` at the repo root.
