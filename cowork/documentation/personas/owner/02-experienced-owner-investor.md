# The experienced owner / investor

## Who they are

Has already built or renovated before — second or third build, or a real
estate investor managing the build remotely (flip, rental). Knows what's
normal and what isn't. May be managing more than one build at the same
time, which brings them close to the
[growing contractor](../contractor/02-growing-contractor.md)
in the type of need, despite being a different role.

## Motivations

Efficiency and data, not reassurance. Wants to confirm numbers, not feel
good — their confidence comes from seeing the history and the facts, not
from friendly badges.

## Pain points

- Tools designed for the anxious owner (simplified language, few numbers)
  feel slow and uninformative for this variant.
- If managing the build remotely, they need visibility as good as if they
  were present — photos and video matter more than text.
- If managing more than one build, they feel the same lack of aggregated
  view as the growing contractor.

## Behaviors

- Comfortable with raw numbers, exports, comparison between builds.
- Less frequent but denser — longer sessions, fewer times.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

The opposite of the
[first-time owner](./01-first-time-owner.md) on the
"assisted interpretation vs. raw data" axis. The same feature (e.g. an
"on track" badge) that reassures one may seem condescending or
insufficient to the other — it's a concrete case where the same screen
needs to work for both, not choose one.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: access to detailed numbers always available, even if
  the default view is simplified for the other variant — never hide the
  detail, just don't impose it by default.
- **In favor of**: photos/video as the primary form of remote
  follow-up, not just text — relevant if this owner is not physically
  close to the build.
- **Design note**: this is the clearest proof that "the owner" is not a
  single persona — the same summary screen has to serve two very
  different readings of the same information, which is normally solved
  with progressive disclosure (simple summary by default, detail one
  click away) rather than two separate apps.
