# The formal, independent inspector

## Who they are

Hired directly by the [owner](../owner/), independent of the
[contractor](../contractor/) — the relationship is structurally one of
verification, not comfortable collaboration. It exists precisely so it can
disagree with the contractor without a conflict of interest.

## Motivations

Maintain professional credibility through objective, defensible records —
what they write can be used to justify decisions to either party.

## Pain points

- If the non-conformities they record can be closed unilaterally by the
  contractor without their verification, their role loses its purpose.

## Behaviors

- Periodic, formal site visits, structured reports, systematic photographic
  evidence.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike [informal inspection](./02-informal-inspection.md), the power to
block has to be real and technically guaranteed, not just social.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: the state flow already identified in
  [personas.md](../../personas.md) — `OPEN → CORRECTED → VERIFIED` — with
  final verification restricted to whoever opened the non-conformity.
  Without this, this variant won't trust the tool for the one thing it
  needs most from it.
