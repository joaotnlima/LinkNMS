# The Rare-Specialty Subcontractor

## Who they are

A specialty with few qualified crews on the market (e.g. certified
HVAC, specific technical systems). Has more negotiating power than the
other variants — doesn't depend as much on pleasing the contractor to get
more work.

## Motivations

Being efficient with his own time, which is scarce and expensive. Has no
patience for tools that don't clearly pay back the time they demand.

## Pain points

- Any friction in the tool can simply be refused — unlike other variants,
  this one has room to say "I won't use this, call me on the phone."

## Behaviors

- Minimal use by choice, not for lack of digital ease — the difference
  from the [independent tradesperson](./01-independent-tradesperson.md)
  is the reason behind the similar behavior: one doesn't know/doesn't
  want to learn, the other decides it isn't worth his time.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

He is the variant with the most power to refuse to use the tool at all.
The other two variants use it because they're asked to; this one only
uses it if he thinks it's worth it.

## Implications for features

- **Risk to take seriously**: if the tool for subcontractors isn't
  genuinely faster than a phone call, this variant simply won't use it —
  and his progress record falls outside the audit log, which undermines
  the product's core purpose exactly in the most expensive specialties
  with the highest risk of dispute.
- **In favor of**: making sure the "cost" of using the app for this
  persona (time, clicks) is always lower than the immediate value he
  gets (a simple confirmation, without re-explaining over the phone).
