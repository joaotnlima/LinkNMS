# The Independent Tradesperson

## Who they are

Electrician, plumber, or similar, working alone, with no crew of his own.
Comes onto the site for a short window, leaves, and doesn't think about
it again until the next task.

## Motivations

Knowing what to do and when, with the least possible interaction with
tools that aren't the work itself.

## Pain points

- Zero patience for anything that looks like bureaucracy — if the tool
  asks for more than "mark done" and a photo, it's friction
  disproportionate to the value he gets from it.

## Behaviors

- Extremely occasional use — comes in, sees the task, marks it, leaves.
  Never goes exploring the app.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike the [small specialized crew](./02-small-specialized-crew.md), he
has no one managing coordination on his behalf — he himself decides
whether to accept the proposed task.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: the most minimal interface of any persona in the
  product — see task, mark progress/complete, take a photo. Nothing
  more. This reinforces the gap already identified in
  [personas.md](../../personas.md): access by task (`TaskAssignment`),
  not by entire project.
