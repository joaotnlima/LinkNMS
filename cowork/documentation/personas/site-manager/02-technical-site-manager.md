# The Technical/Trained Site Manager

## Who they are

Junior civil engineer or similar, hired to manage day-to-day execution, a
bridge between the site and the
[contractor](../contractor/)/[owner](../owner/). Closer, in background,
to an ADMIN-level site manager than to the
[field foreman](./01-field-foreman.md).

## Motivations

To be seen as a competent professional, to manage well technically, to
prepare reports that justify decisions to the owner.

## Pain points

- Lack of aggregated data to justify decisions or anticipate problems
  (timeline/budget deviations before they become a crisis).

## Behaviors

- Comfortable with dashboards, reports, data — more similar, behaviorally,
  to the
  [experienced owner/investor](../owner/02-experienced-owner-investor.md)
  than to the foreman he replaces in the role.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike the [field foreman](./01-field-foreman.md), this one wants to see
trends and reports, not just today's task list.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: exportable reports (a gap already identified in the
  roadmap) and a view of budget/timeline deviation trends over time, not
  just the current value.
