# The Field-Promoted Foreman

## Who they are

Came up from hands-on practice — former electrician, former mason,
promoted to foreman through experience, not management training. Strong
at solving problems on site, not very comfortable with admin work or
digital tools.

## Motivations

That the build progresses without hiccups and that the crews know what to
do without him having to repeat instructions constantly.

## Pain points

- "Office-style" management tools feel distant from his real work, which
  is coordinating people who are physically present.
- Reporting upward (to the [contractor](../contractor/)) eats time he'd
  rather spend solving real problems.

## Behaviors

- Physically on site all day — the phone is the only realistic device,
  much like the
  [traditional master builder](../contractor/01-traditional-master-builder.md),
  whose usage profile he closely shares.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike the [technical site manager](./02-technical-site-manager.md), he
has no interest in reports or aggregated data — he wants clear task lists
and little else.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: simple checklists per crew/day, not long forms — the
  same logic as the traditional master builder.
- **Against**: any interface that looks like a management dashboard
  instead of a task list.
