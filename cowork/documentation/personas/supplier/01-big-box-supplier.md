# The big-box supplier

## Who they are

Supplies standardized materials (building-materials store, large
distributor). The delivery process is already relatively predictable and
standardized on their end.

## Motivations

Fulfill orders efficiently, without customized processes per client.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike the
[critical/custom-order supplier](./02-critical-custom-order-supplier.md), a
one-off delay rarely has a serious cascading effect on the schedule — there
are alternatives.

## Implications for features

- **Note**: low interest in custom integration — for this variant, a
  simple order/delivery confirmation (once "Purchase orders" is built)
  probably suffices. Not where design effort should be invested first.
