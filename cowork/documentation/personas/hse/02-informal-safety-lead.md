# The informal safety lead

## Who they are

On small builds, with no legal obligation for a dedicated HSE technician,
it's often the [foreman](../site-manager/01-field-foreman.md) themselves
who informally takes on this function, without specialized training.

## Motivations

Avoid accidents on site, without formal processes behind it — it's
practical common sense, not compliance.

## Pain points

- No time or training to document systematically, even when they want to
  do it well.

## Where this differs from other variants of the same role

Unlike the [external technician](./01-external-hse-officer.md), this
variant has no formal recording obligation at all — if the tool demands
the same level of detail for both cases, this variant simply won't record
anything.

## Implications for features

- **In favor of**: incident/risk recording also has to work in a minimal
  version (photo + short description), not only the full formal version —
  the same "don't assume all users of the same role have the same level of
  rigor" logic that appears in
  [Quality Inspection](../quality-inspection/02-informal-inspection.md).
