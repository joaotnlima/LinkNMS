How the flow maps to roadmap §4.3 acceptance criteria

  • AC 2 — Decision log stamps author + timestamp on every entry; edits show a visible “edited / rev” marker (append-only, revision-preserving). Surface 2.
  • AC 3 — Any party can raise a change order with a cost delta; it opens as ● Proposed. Surfaces 2 & 3.
  • AC 4 — Approve/Reject is available only to a party other than the proposer (or the owner); the proposer sees a disabled “waiting” state. The action stamps who + when. Surface 3.
  • AC 5 — Current budget = baseline + Σ approved deltas, each contribution individually visible. Surface 4.
  • AC 6 — “Who decided, when, how much” is answerable in one screen. Surfaces 1 & 4.
  • AC 7 — Proposed & rejected COs are shown but excluded from the total. Surface 4.
  • AC 8 — History reads in chronological order with full attribution. Surfaces 2 & 4.
  • AC 9 (added per CEO/PM request) — Progress & forecast on one screen: total work done, cost to date vs. budget, and a predicted completion date, shown as a Gantt or a table (toggle). The forecast is derived from logged progress + approved change orders — a schedule sibling of the budget trail. Surface 5.

Non-technical / on-site requirements addressed

  • One-handed, large targets — primary action is bottom-anchored (52px), all controls ≥ 44px, bottom tab nav within thumb reach.
  • Plain language — “Log a decision”, “Does it change the cost?”, “who decided it, when, and how much it moved the budget” — no jargon.
  • Two-sided clarity — proposer vs. approver is shown by label + position first, Owner-Blue / Builder-Orange second; never colour alone.
  • Record, not chat — approvals render as stamped ledger lines; immutability is tamper-evident (visible edit markers), per the copy guardrails.
  • Brand — House Record mark + “Trust built-in.” lockup, Paper surface, drawing-like rules and tokens from the design system.
